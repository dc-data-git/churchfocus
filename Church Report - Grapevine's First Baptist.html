At a glance

Grapevine’s First Baptist Church is at 301 E. Texas St. in Grapevine, about 0.12 miles away, with Sunday worship listed at 10:30 a.m. The church identifies as Baptist; a public Worship Pastor recruitment listing identifies it as Baptist–SBC and reports weekly attendance of 940. Its stated mission is to serve every generation by meeting people where they are with biblical truth so they may know and follow Jesus. Senior Pastor Doug Page is named in recent sermon material.

How it fits what you asked for

The available evidence matches the requested Baptist tradition and Spanish-language availability. The church explicitly teaches biblical inerrancy, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus, believer’s baptism by immersion, and eternal security; these are stated church beliefs, not inferences from its Baptist identity. Spanish worship is listed Sundays at 10:30 a.m. in the Fellowship Hall, with a 9:15 a.m. Latino community time beforehand. It also lists ministries for children, students, college-aged adults, young professionals, adults, and seniors, along with Life Groups and Wednesday Midweek offerings. A recruitment listing reports attendance of 940, so it may fit a preference for a larger church, though that figure is not an official church annual profile.
